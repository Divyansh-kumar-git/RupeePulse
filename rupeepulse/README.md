# RupeePulse — Gen Z ₹10k Budget & Decision Simulator

RupeePulse is a real-time, zero-delusion personal finance and decision lab engineered for young Indian professionals, students, and tier-1 city grinders navigating a ₹10,000 monthly allowance cycle.

## Core Features

- **Decision Lab (What-If Simulator)**: Simulate swiping on Swiggy, booking concert tickets, or unplanned splurges before spending a single rupee. Live metrics show daily allowance deltas, regret days, and shock tolerance without altering your baseline until committed.
- **Pillar-by-Pillar Health Score**: Transparent 0–100 health index decomposed into 4 pillars (Runway, Budget Pacing, Bill Coverage, and Buffer Resilience).
- **Dual-Pacing Burndown Forecast**: Real-time trajectory plotting both current burn pace and strict safe-allowance adherence with an explicit Day 30 deficit / cushion forecast.
- **Goal Funding & Sinking Funds**: Per-goal daily contribution pace, deadline monitoring (e.g. Earphones sinking fund, Emergency Shield), and accurate goal-dipping calculations.
- **Interactive Scrubber**: Seamless 30-day time machine to jump to kickoff, payday, bill deadlines, or broke week.
- **Local Persistence & Zero Setup**: Fully persistent state in localStorage with one-click reset to defaults.
