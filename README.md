# 💸 RupeePulse

### Make Every Rupee Count!

RupeePulse is an interactive **₹10,000 Budget & Decision Simulator** designed for Gen Z. It helps users explore financial decisions, understand budgeting, and see how different spending choices affect their available money.

## ✨ Features

- 💰 **Budget Simulation:** Explore how to manage a ₹10,000 budget.
- 📊 **Spending Decisions:** Understand the impact of different financial choices.
- 🎯 **Interactive Experience:** Experiment with different budgeting scenarios.
- 🧠 **Financial Awareness:** Encourage smarter spending habits and better money management.
- 👥 **Gen Z Focused:** Designed around the financial decisions and everyday spending needs of young people.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of RupeePulse is to make budgeting more engaging and accessible by turning everyday financial decisions into an interactive learning experience.
